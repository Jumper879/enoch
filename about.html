how I think

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

(01)

Research Before Design

Every project starts with research—understanding the market, competitors, and target audience. This ensures design decisions are intentional and grounded in context, not assumptions.

(02)

Clarifying What Clients Can't Express

Many clients struggle to articulate what they truly need. I help uncover the real problem through analysis of past data, timelines, user behavior, and brand goals—then design solutions around that clarity.

(03)

User-First Thinking

Aesthetics matter, but usability and experience come first. I design with the end user in mind, ensuring clarity, ease of interaction, and emotional connection before visual polish.
